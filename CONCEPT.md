# Bropilot

Bropilot is a studio for making a system's shared knowledge visible.

It helps a team collect and connect what is known about a system across three lenses:

- **Foundations** — what the system is for, who it serves, the needs and constraints that shape it, and the outcomes it seeks.
- **Domain** — the language, concepts, behaviours, events, and journeys that describe the world the system operates in.
- **Implementation** — the code, interfaces, services, and evidence that realise the system.

The result is a navigable knowledge graph: a durable, portable representation of the relationships between intent, domain understanding, and implementation. It should give people and their AI collaborators one clear source of truth for understanding a system and deciding what to do next.
